the letter

Hey.

I don't really know how to start this because I know there probably aren't enough words that can fix what I did to you.

I am truly sorry.

I am sorry for cheating on you. I am sorry for betraying your trust, for lying to you, and for destroying something that you gave your heart to. You trusted me, you loved me, and I was the person who was supposed to protect what we had. Instead, I was the one who broke it.

There is no excuse for what I did.

I could explain so many things. I could tell you what was going through my head or what I was thinking at the time. But none of those things would change the truth.

I cheated on you.

I hurt you.

I broke your heart.

And you never deserved any of it.

What hurts me the most is knowing that we barely even had the chance to experience everything we were supposed to experience together.

We only really had that one day together.

One day.

And even though it was such a short time, that day meant so much to me. I still remember being able to actually be beside you instead of just seeing you through a screen. I remember when you had to leave because you were going to a concert, and then after that you had to go back to Australia.

It was such a short time, but somehow that one day became one of the memories I held onto the most.

I thought there would be more.

I thought someday I would be the one going to Australia.

I thought I would finally get the chance to spend more than just one day with you.

I thought the distance between us was only temporary.

You were going to stay there, and my plan was to go there too.

That was the future I was looking toward.

Not some perfect life.

Not some imaginary movie.

Just you and me finally being close enough that we wouldn't have to keep missing each other through a phone.

And I destroyed the chance of that happening.

That is something I don't think I will ever stop feeling guilty about.

You gave me something real, and I still chose to risk it.

You trusted me, and I betrayed you.

You loved me, and I made you regret loving me.

I know saying sorry cannot undo any of that.

I know I cannot ask you to forgive me.

I know I cannot ask you to understand me.

And I cannot ask you to give me another chance.

You don't owe me any of those things.

I just needed to tell you that I am sorry.

I'm sorry for every time I made you feel like you weren't enough.

You were enough.

You were never the problem.

I was.

It was my decisions.

My selfishness.

My lack of self control.

My inability to understand the value of the person I had in front of me until I was the one who destroyed everything.

And I know I don't get to decide how you remember me.

Maybe when you think of me, you will remember the good things.

Maybe you will remember the bad.

Maybe you will only remember the person who cheated on you.

And honestly, I won't blame you.

I know what I did.

I know the kind of person I became through those choices.

I cheated on you, and that is something I have to carry.

I also don't want to make this letter about asking you to come back.

I don't want to use my sadness to make you feel guilty.

I don't want to tell you how much I'm hurting and expect you to comfort me when I am the reason you are hurting in the first place.

That would be selfish too.

So I'm not asking for anything.

I am only saying goodbye.

I am not going to message you again.

Not because I stopped caring about you.

I care about you so much.

That is exactly why I need to stop.

I don't want my name showing up on your phone and making you remember everything again.

I don't want to keep reopening the wound that I caused.

I don't want to keep disturbing your peace just because I miss you.

Missing you is my problem now.

The regret is mine.

The guilt is mine.

The memories are mine.

And I have to learn how to live with all of it without putting any of that weight back onto you.

I wish I could go back.

I wish I could go back to that one day we finally had together and know that it would be one of the last real memories I would ever have of us.

I would have held onto every second differently.

I would have appreciated you differently.

I would have understood just how precious that little bit of time was.

But I can't go back.

All I can do is accept what I did and accept that I lost someone who truly cared about me.

Thank you for loving me.

Thank you for giving me a chance.

Thank you for the conversations, the time, the little things, the calls, the messages, and especially that one day we finally got to be together.

Thank you for making me believe that maybe one day I really would get to be beside you in Australia.

I wish I could have made that future happen the right way.

I wish I could have been better for you.

I wish the person you loved had been someone who knew how to protect your heart.

I'm sorry that person wasn't me.

And I am sorry that the person who should have made you feel safe became the person who hurt you.

So this is me letting you go.

You don't have to reply.

You don't have to forgive me.

You don't have to remember me kindly.

Just live your life and be happy.

Go wherever life takes you.

And hopefully, one day, the pain I caused you will become something that no longer hurts.

As for me, I will remember you.

And I will remember what I did.

I don't want to rewrite the story to make myself look better.

I don't want to pretend I was a victim.

I wasn't.

I was the one who broke the heart of someone I loved.

So when you remember me, maybe the truth is enough.

I was the guy who loved you.

I was also the guy who cheated on you.

The guy who had something real and still managed to destroy it.

And I'm deeply sorry.

I love you enough to finally leave you in peace.

Goodbye.

I'm sorry.
